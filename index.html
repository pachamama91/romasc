About the Club

Formed in 2026, ROMA SC is a community-driven football club competing in the Kenyan Division 1 League. We focus on talent, discipline, and representing the spirit of our community.

History

We formed ROMA SC in 2026. It stands for Rongena/Manaret Sporting Club. As a community club, legends from the former Kisabei FC came together to form this team to compete at the professional level. We are proudly based at the Kisabei Grounds.
