About the Club

Formed in 2026, ROMA SC is a community-driven football club dedicated to professional excellence in the Kenyan Division 1 League.

We proudly represent the communities of Rongena and Manaret from our home base at Kisabei Grounds.

Our History

ROMA SC was founded in 2026 by legends from the former Kisabei FC.

The club was created to provide a professional football pathway for talented young players and to build a lasting sporting legacy in the region.
