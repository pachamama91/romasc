About the Club

ROMA SC (Rongena/Manaret Sporting Club) is a modern community football club founded in the year 2026 with a vision of building a strong football culture, empowering local youth talent, and creating a professional sporting identity for the Rongena and Manaret communities.

The club was established by passionate football lovers, respected community leaders, and former legends associated with the historic Kisabei FC. Their shared dream was to create a disciplined and competitive football institution capable of competing at the highest levels of Kenyan football while still remaining deeply connected to the grassroots community.

ROMA SC proudly calls the famous Kisabei Grounds its home. The grounds have become a symbol of unity, determination, and sporting passion where players, fans, and the local community gather to support the growth of football in the region.

The club operates under strong values of discipline, teamwork, professionalism, respect, hard work, and sportsmanship. Through football, ROMA SC aims to nurture young talent, create career opportunities for players, and inspire the next generation of athletes within Bomet County and across Kenya.

Since its formation, the club has continued to attract talented players, loyal supporters, and committed management members who believe in the long-term vision of building one of the most respected football institutions in the country.

ROMA SC proudly competes in the Kenyan Division 1 structure with ambitions of earning promotion into higher national competitions in the near future. The club’s identity is represented by the powerful Phoenix symbol — a sign of rebirth, resilience, strength, ambition, and rising above challenges.

Our History

ROMA SC traces its roots back to the strong football traditions of the Rongena and Manaret regions. The club was officially founded in 2026 after several former Kisabei FC legends and football stakeholders came together with a common goal of reviving competitive football within the community.

What started as a community football idea quickly transformed into a serious sporting project focused on professionalism, youth development, and long-term football success.
