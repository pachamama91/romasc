About the Club

ROMA SC (Rongena/Manaret Sporting Club) is a modern football institution founded in 2026 with a strong vision of nurturing football talent, promoting discipline, strengthening community unity, and building a professional sporting culture within the Rongena and Manaret regions. The club was established by legendary former players of Kisabei FC, a team that left a lasting mark in local football and inspired generations of young footballers within the community. After years of success, experience, and service at Kisabei FC, the founders came together with a shared dream of creating a new football identity that would unite the people and provide a platform for upcoming talents to grow and compete at higher levels. ROMA SC was therefore born not only as a football club, but also as a community-driven movement dedicated to inspiring youth, promoting positive social values, and representing the pride, passion, and spirit of the people of Rongena and Manaret. The club aims to compete in the Kenyan football league system while maintaining strong roots within the local community. Through structured training, mentorship, teamwork, and professionalism, ROMA SC seeks to develop players capable of excelling both on and off the pitch. The club also believes in using football as a tool for social transformation by encouraging unity, discipline, leadership, and healthy living among young people. Built on the legacy and experience of former Kisabei FC legends, ROMA SC carries forward a winning mentality, resilience, and commitment to excellence. The club envisions becoming one of the most respected and successful community football clubs in the region, while creating opportunities for talented players to showcase their abilities at county, national, and even international levels. With passionate supporters, committed management, and a growing pool of talented players, ROMA SC stands as a symbol of hope, ambition, and the future of football development in the community.

Our History

ROMA SC was officially founded in 2026 after a group of respected former Kisabei FC legends came together with a shared vision of reviving competitive football within the Rongena and Manaret regions. Having spent many years representing the community with pride, passion, and success during their playing careers at Kisabei FC, the founders recognized the growing need for a new football institution that would continue the legacy of community football while creating fresh opportunities for the younger generation. For many years, football had been one of the strongest sources of unity, talent development, and entertainment within the area. Kisabei FC had become a symbol of determination and excellence, producing talented players and creating unforgettable memories for supporters across the region. However, as time passed, many former players and football lovers felt there was a need to build a more organized, modern, and sustainable football structure that could compete at higher levels within the Kenyan football system. Driven by their love for the game and commitment to the community, the former Kisabei FC players united to establish ROMA SC — Rongena/Manaret Sporting Club. The club was founded not only to compete and win matches, but also to restore football pride within the community and provide young talented players with a clear pathway to grow, develop, and showcase their abilities. From its formation, ROMA SC embraced values of discipline, teamwork, professionalism, respect, and unity. The founders believed that football could serve as a powerful tool for social transformation by bringing people together, mentoring youth, reducing negative social influences, and inspiring ambition among young people in the region. Their goal was to create a club that would belong to the people — a true community club supported by local fans, leaders, and football lovers. The name ROMA SC represents the unity of Rongena and Manaret communities, symbolizing togetherness, shared identity, and collective ambition. Since its establishment, the club has continued to attract talented players, passionate supporters, and dedicated management members who share the dream of seeing the team rise through the ranks of Kenyan football. Although still young, ROMA SC carries the experience, spirit, and winning mentality inherited from the legendary Kisabei FC generation. The club continues to build a strong foundation focused on long-term success, youth empowerment, and community development. With determination, vision, and the unwavering support of the people, ROMA SC aims to become one of the most respected and successful grassroots football clubs in the region and beyond.
