About the Club

Formed in 2026, ROMA SC is more than a football team; it is a movement rooted in the community of Kisabei. We are dedicated to competing at the highest level of the Kenyan Division 1 League while fostering local talent and sporting excellence.

Our History

ROMA SC was born in 2026 from a shared vision of unity and legacy. Legends from the former Kisabei FC joined forces to build a professional platform for the youth of Rongena and Manaret. Our journey began at the historic Kisabei Grounds, which remains our fortress and home today.
