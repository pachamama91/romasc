About the Club

Formed in 2026, ROMA SC is a community-driven football club dedicated to professional excellence in the Kenyan Division 1 League. Based at the Kisabei Grounds, we represent the talent and passion of our local community.

History

ROMA SC (Rongena/Manaret Sporting Club) was founded in 2026 as a community club. We were formed by Legends from the former Kisabei FC who came together to create a professional path for the next generation of footballers. We are proud to call the Kisabei Grounds our home.
