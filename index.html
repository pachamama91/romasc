About the Club

Formed in 2026, ROMA SC is a community-driven football club dedicated to professional excellence in the Kenyan Division 1 League. We are proud to represent our local community from our base at the Kisabei Grounds.

Our History

ROMA SC was founded in 2026 by Legends from the former Kisabei FC. We came together to build a professional path for the next generation of footballers in the Rongena and Manaret areas.
