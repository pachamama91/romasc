About the Club

Formed in the year 2026, ROMA SC is a community-driven football club dedicated to excellence, talent development, and sportsmanship in the Kenyan Division 1 league.

History

In 2026, we formed ROMA SC (Rongena/Manaret Sporting Club). It is a community club born out of passion and legacy. Legends from the former Kisabei FC came together to form the club to compete at a high level in the Kenyan Division 1 league. The club proudly calls the Kisabei Grounds its home base.
